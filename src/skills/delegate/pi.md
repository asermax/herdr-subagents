When a child needs you, your extension wakes you automatically. It spawns `{{helper}} watch` once per session and forwards each status change, so you never arm a wake yourself: a finished turn (`done`, or an `idle` that ends a turn) wakes you to collect, `blocked` wakes you to surface the dialog to the human, the resume from a block wakes you so you know they answered, and a lost child (`gone`) wakes you too.
